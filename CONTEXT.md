# IDEAS Branding

The visual identity of IDEAS (Intelligent Data Exploration Assistant for Science), the visualization thrust of Argonne Base: its logo family, colours, typography, and the presentation template built from them.

## Language

### Logo family

**Logo**:
The family name for every official IDEAS mark: the Wordmark, the Lockups, and the Icon.
_Avoid_: brand, emblem

**Voxel**:
One square cell of the logo grid. Filled Voxels take their colour from the Transfer-Function Ramp.
_Avoid_: pixel, block, square

**Hollow Voxel**:
A voxel drawn as an outline only, meaning a sample skipped by sparse sampling. Part of the Wordmark and the Lockups at every size. The Icon has none: at favicon sizes its tittle must be solid.
_Avoid_: empty cell, gap, outline

**Wordmark**:
"IDEAS" spelled in Voxels, with nothing else.
_Avoid_: logotype, text logo

**Tagline**:
"intelligent data exploration assistant for science", always lowercase inside the Logo. Running text uses the Title Case expansion.
_Avoid_: subtitle, slogan

**Horizontal Lockup**:
The Wordmark with the Tagline set beside it on three lines, as in the website header.
_Avoid_: logo + text, header logo, banner

**Stacked Lockup**:
The Wordmark with the Tagline set beneath it, for square spaces.
_Avoid_: vertical logo, square logo

**Icon**:
A single voxel "i", for favicons and avatars. Its Transfer-Function Ramp runs top to bottom, not left to right.
_Avoid_: favicon (that is one use of the Icon), monogram, symbol

**Volume Grid**:
The faint dot pattern of empty voxel positions behind a large Wordmark. Decoration for hero uses only, never part of the Logo.
_Avoid_: background dots, texture

### Colour and type

**Transfer-Function Ramp**:
The seven-step colour sequence (navy through blue, sky, teal and lime to amber) that colours the Wordmark's Voxels from left to right (the Icon's from top to bottom).
_Avoid_: gradient, rainbow, colormap (in brand docs)

**Ink**:
The deep navy (`#0b1f3a`) used for text on light backgrounds and as the dark background colour.
_Avoid_: black, dark blue

**Background**:
The light off-white (`#f7f8fb`) behind content slides and light pages.
_Avoid_: white, paper, light grey

**Theme Accents**:
The six chart and accent colours of the Slide Template, taken from the Transfer-Function Ramp in a fixed order chosen so neighbouring chart series stay distinguishable.
_Avoid_: chart palette, series colours

### Presentations

**Slide Template**:
The official IDEAS PowerPoint template used for every IDEAS presentation.
_Avoid_: deck, theme, master

**Partner Strip**:
The full-colour row of institution logos in the slide footer: Argonne (the lead lab) first, then the university Partners alphabetically by full name, then Kitware, then Veeda AI last. Shown large on the Navy Layouts and small on the Light Layouts.
_Avoid_: logo bar, sponsor row

**Navy Layout**:
A Slide Template layout with the Ink background and the large Partner Strip on a white band across the bottom: Navy Title, Section Divider and Closing.
_Avoid_: dark layout, title slide (for the set)

**Light Layout**:
Any other Slide Template layout: the Background, with the content-slide footer (the Wordmark at left, the small Partner Strip in the centre, the slide number at right).
_Avoid_: content slide (for the set), white layout

**Partner**:
An external institution contributing to IDEAS: University of Nebraska–Lincoln, UC Davis, University of Cincinnati, UIC, Kitware, Veeda AI. Argonne leads IDEAS and is not a Partner, but it appears first in the Partner Strip.
_Avoid_: sponsor, collaborator
